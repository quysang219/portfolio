02 The problem I solve

Security data lives in fragmented email threads, Teams chats and Excel sheets.

Two recurring gaps the program ran into — one on the analytics side, one on the workflow side. Each became a platform; each gets its own case study below.

Problem · 01

Coverage tracked, posture invisible.

Most security reports tell you today's compliance number — not the trend behind it, not the drift week-over-week, not the silent failure hiding inside the percentage. A centralized data layer with snapshot history turns the same numbers into posture over time: progress recorded, anomalies flagged early, recognition surfaced from the data rather than from memory.

Read the case study →
Problem · 02

Owners can read, but can't act.

Comments, exceptions, plans — everything that explains the status — still flow through emails, Teams chats and Excel sheets. Why isn't the antivirus on SRV-PROD-04 running? Was an exception approved, by whom and when? A workflow surface lets owners record the answer in-line, where the data lives, instead of scattering it across inboxes.
