About

Pipelined Compliance

As infrastructure and applications scale by the seconds, how do we reliably detect and protect?

Over the years, I observed that security requirements reached System Owners through multiple fragmented channels, with execution managed through emails, chats, and spreadsheets. Escalations and audits frequently occurred without the full context of related security activities across domains, making it difficult to understand, quantify, and continuously measure an organization's true security posture. As infrastructure becomes increasingly agile, elastic, and automated, the way we secure it must evolve just as rapidly.

To address these challenges, my work has focused on transforming fragmented security operations into a connected, data-driven ecosystem. By partnering with security teams, I capture their requirements, engineer them into scalable workflows, connect activities across security domains, build analytics capabilities that surface context instantly, create a unified platform for System Owners, and expose meaningful metrics that make security posture measurable, transparent, and actionable for managers.

Built a scalable vulnerability management operating model that enabled compliance through risk-based governance and self-service remediation.

Quarterly vulnerability scanning was a regulatory requirement under Circular 09, but compliance depended on more than simply running scans. Findings were delivered in large batches, ownership was unclear, and security teams became the bottleneck for validating remediation, creating unnecessary delays and limiting visibility into overall progress.

I designed and implemented the end-to-end operating model, including risk-based prioritization, remediation SLAs aligned to asset criticality, ownership tracking, and a self-service rescan capability that allowed application teams to validate fixes independently. By standardizing the workflow and making progress transparent, remediation became a predictable process rather than a series of manual follow-ups, allowing security to focus on governance instead of operational coordination.

Sustained over 95% regulatory compliance across quarterly vulnerability assessment cycles while significantly reducing manual coordination through standardized governance and self-service verification.

Built a continuous endpoint compliance program that combined operational visibility with auditable exception management.

Security agents only provide protection when they are actively running and reporting correctly, yet coverage metrics alone rarely explain why machines remain unprotected. Teams relied on fragmented communication to justify exceptions, while genuine product defects became indistinguishable from operational negligence.

I designed a centralized operating model that combined fleet-wide compliance monitoring, owner accountability, and a writeback portal where system owners could document remediation progress and approved exceptions. By investigating residual anomalies rather than accepting them as non-compliance, we also identified defects within the security agent itself and drove vendor remediation.

Achieved 95% endpoint coverage while ensuring every remaining gap was investigated, documented, and governed rather than left unexplained.

Established continuous sensitive-data discovery to reduce PCI DSS exposure before it became an audit finding.

Sensitive information rarely leaks through deliberate misuse—it accumulates through operational activities such as exports, debugging, and temporary storage. Point-in-time audits identify existing exposures but provide little insight into whether new risks are continuously emerging.

I implemented a continuous discovery and remediation process that monitored servers for unauthorized cardholder data, tracked remediation with system owners, and highlighted newly appearing exposures as early indicators of upstream process failures. This shifted the focus from cleaning historical findings to preventing repeated data leakage.

Reduced cardholder-data exposure across the environment while transforming sensitive-data protection into an ongoing operational process.

User account reviews were constrained by manual effort, requiring analysts to consolidate extracts from multiple platforms before meaningful analysis could begin. As a result, stale accounts and excessive privileges often remained undetected between review cycles.

Enabled frequent access reviews through automation, reducing the exposure window for dormant and excessive privileged accounts.
