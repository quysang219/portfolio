About

Security operations, measured.

Cyber GRC Analyst · VPBank · 8 years in the bank, 4 in security

For four years at VPBank, I have turned scattered security signals — vulnerability scans, agent telemetry, hardening checks, firewall and account reviews — into a single, measured picture of the bank's posture, then built the workflows that close the gaps the numbers expose.

My method is a continuous loop: observe what's actually happening, measure it as a defensible trend, build the engine that lets owners act, and watch the metric move. Coverage that was tracked but invisible becomes a number an executive can read — and a team can improve.
